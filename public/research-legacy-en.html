04 / FORENSICS

Tracing centers on “address→entity” attribution and probabilistic links

Blockchains reveal flows but do not automatically reveal owners' names. Real-name attribution becomes stronger where chain-specific heuristics intersect with exchange KYC, law enforcement data, and OSINT.

UTXO / BITCOIN

Common input ownership + change detection

  1. CIOH assumes that multiple inputs in the same transaction belong to one entity
  2. Estimate change candidates using new addresses, non-standard denominations, and matching script types
  3. Strengthen clusters through subsequent convergence patterns
  4. Suspend or relax heuristics when CoinJoin is detected

Caution Publicly documented cases show that naive application can produce error rates above 63%, making exception handling essential. Foundational research

ACCOUNT / EVM

Call tracing + event reconstruction

  1. Combine external transactions, internal calls, and ERC-20 Transfer logs
  2. Decode DEX swap, smart contract, and bridge events
  3. Detect fan-in/out, peeling, sweep patterns
  4. Strengthen relationships using nonce, gas sponsors, and common counterparties

Industry standards This is a technical reconstruction based on public forensic materials, rather than TranSight's proprietary implementation.

BRIDGE / CROSS-CHAIN

Match events on both chains

  1. Match lock/mint or burn/mint events
  2. Directly link bridge message and nonce
  3. Use time/value correlation when direct references are absent
  4. Strengthen links using gas payers, recipient address reuse, and aggregator events

Industry standards Public materials indicate that probabilistic matching using fee deductions and time windows is common.

Demixing does not “decrypt” the cryptography

01 POOLBuild candidate deposit/withdrawal sets within the same fixed-denomination pool
02 TIMEAssess deposit-withdrawal temporal proximity and FIFO similarity
03 VALUECompare fee-adjusted amounts, denomination combinations, and splitting patterns
04 FINGERPRINTCombine gas, relayers, withdrawal schedules, and address reuse
05 EXITTrace post-withdrawal identification points such as fund consolidation and exchange inflows

Conclusion: This process narrows candidate sets and accumulates probability scores. It does not break the cryptographic separation between deposits and withdrawals provided by zk-SNARKs. The study released in October 2025 (arXiv:2510.09433) was withdrawn by its author on November 18, 2025 due to reference errors, and no revised version had been released as of November 2026. Accordingly, the $2.3 billion linkage figure is presented solely as a claim from the withdrawn paper. 05 / ALGORITHMS

Inside the algorithms: combining evidence beyond a single rule

Commercial products' internal formulas are undisclosed. The analysis below separately examines signals verified in academic papers and public implementations, and the general architecture through which operational systems combine those signals.

Verified principles

UTXO clusters: union-find + exception gates

for tx in blocks:
if not CoinJoin(tx): union(all input addresses)
change = argmax P(output is change | features)
add_edge(cluster, change, confidence, provenance)
  1. CIOH: Assume common control of input addresses in the same transaction and merge disjoint sets.
  2. Change candidates: Score new addresses, script types matching inputs, non-standard amounts, output positions, and locktime/RBF changes.
  3. Exception gates: Block hard merges for CoinJoin/PayJoin, and retain weak relationships as candidate edges with sources and supporting evidence.
  4. Transitive expansion: Expand entity coverage through overlapping input sets and subsequent peel chain patterns.

The simulation study reported average error rates of 63.46% for multi-input alone, 92.66% for one-time change alone, and 57.47% combined. These are not measures of commercial system precision, but warning figures dependent on the simulation and error definitions. Clustering · Error study

05A / VENDOR STACK

Redrawing the map along commercial vendors’ actual product boundaries

KYT provides alerts at deposit and withdrawal, while Investigator expands the graph after an alert. The two interfaces share the same attribution, cluster, and risk logic even when separate. Only features disclosed in public documentation are compared; internal weights are left unspecified.

Node, RPC ingestion

Blocks, transactions, logs, and trace data are loaded into the raw area. The public GraphSense implementation uses Cassandra’s {NETWORK}_raw to store block, transaction, exchange_rates.

Graphs by chain

UTXO is converted into a transaction/address graph, and account chains into token transfer, call graph representations. Spark computes the address graph and denormalized view.

Clusters, attribution

The public implementation confirms co-spend union-find and CoinJoin exclusion. Commercial vendors do not disclose their full lists of heuristics.

Exposure, behavior detection

Combines direct/indirect exposure, direction, amount, and sanctions lists with patterns such as peeling, layering, change of custody.

KYT alerts

Builds queues based on rules and thresholds and continuously reevaluates them. Chainalysis officially describes alerts within seconds of on-chain activity.

Investigator expansion

Expands forward and backward flows, hidden connections, multi-route path, swap, bridge in the investigation graph.

Leverage point

Identifies third parties such as VASP, custodian that hold information about address controllers. This is an official term in TRM’s law enforcement guide.

Legal process

Obtains KYC, IP, and account records through subpoenas, warrants, MLAT, and freezing requests. An on-chain graph alone does not establish a real name.

Off-chain cross-checking

Cross-checks against server, device, payment record, and administrator evidence to strengthen or refute attribution.

Evidence export

Preserves the graph, timestamp, label, sources, audit logs in a reproducible report.

Official TRM procedure

A–D investigation loop

  1. A Input: Enter tx hash, incoming/outgoing address into a tracing tool
  2. B Mapping: Expand the transaction network to identify a leverage point
  3. C Third parties: Identify the VASP, custodian holding address controller information
  4. D Legal process: Obtain KYC, account, IP records from third parties

Operating principle: Identify illicit conduct → Trace proceeds → Seek disruption → Serve legal process. This is the missing procedure between a tool’s screen and actual identity confirmation. Official Flip Book

Practical assessment gate

A graph edge does not directly identify a person

The sequence must be primary observation(tx, event, trace) → machine inference(cluster, exposure, signature) → analyst review(alternative hypotheses, false positive exceptions) → independent corroboration(multiple tools, manual reproduction) → legal attribution(KYC, servers, devices). Skipping intermediate stages can lead to mistaking “the same address cluster” for “the same natural person.”

06 / PRIVACY SYSTEMS

Even on privacy chains, distinguish “what is hidden and what remains”

Cryptographically concealed fields differ from clues left at boundaries and in networks and behavior. Probabilistic estimation does not mean plaintext recovery or personal identification.

Monero

1 real input among 16 ring members

  • Ring signature: 1 real input + 15 decoy inputs. A key image prevents double spending without revealing the real input.
  • RingCT: Hides amounts with Pedersen commitment and uses Bulletproofs+ range proofs to verify conservation of value.
  • Stealth address: Generates a one-time address from the recipient’s public key, keeping the actual receiving address off-chain.
  • Dandelion++: A stem phase along a few paths followed by fluff diffusion weakens first-spy IP estimation.

OSPEAD reported an average MAP decoder success rate of 23.5% under the existing distribution (nominally 1/16=6.25%), with an effective ring size of 4.2. Estimates under the proposed distribution are 7.6% and 13.2. These represent average best guesses, rather than confirmation for individual rings. OSPEAD

Zcash

note commitment and nullifier are not directly linked

  • A note commitment conceals value, recipient, randomness and enters the Merkle tree.
  • Spending reveals a nullifier to prevent double spending, but it is not directly linked to the commitment.
  • A zk proof proves note membership, spending key, nullifier calculation, and conservation of value.
  • The net value, time, public addresses at the transparent↔shielded boundary are visible, but the sender, recipient, individual amounts within z→z are not.

Boundary analysis in a 2018 study of Sprout usage claimed anonymity-set reductions of 65.6% from founder/miner patterns and 3.5% from general heuristics, totaling 69.1%. These are era- and pool-dependent figures that cannot be directly applied to current Orchard and other pools. Kappos et al.

07 / CAPABILITY

Current capabilities: “observation→risk assessment→case development” are strengthening

However, interpreting this as “real-time comprehensive surveillance→automatic real-name confirmation→automatic taxation” is an exaggeration. Public evidence and bottlenecks are separated for each stage.

Transaction flow observationStrong

Public-chain transaction graphs are reproducible. BTC, ETH flow visualization and address clustering were specified in procurement reporting.

Complex pattern detectionUnder construction

A system applying AI, ML, statistics to 8 billion transaction statements annually and combining age, gender, region and transaction-type data is scheduled for completion at the end of 2026.

Address–real-name attributionConditional

Stronger when linked to exchange KYC, law enforcement records, test deposits/withdrawals, public addresses. On-chain data alone cannot confirm the owner.

Mixer backtracingQuantitative performance unknown

Listed as a product feature, but proprietary algorithms, success rates, false positives, false negatives, and case-level results have not been disclosed.

Cross-chainScope unknown

Support for over 100 chains cannot be verified because the original request for proposals has not been obtained. The specific list of supported chains is also undisclosed.

Compulsory enforcementExchange-centered

For domestic exchange accounts, withdrawal and return claims — not private keys — are attached so the assets can be frozen and sold. The same method does not work for self-custody.

08 / BLIND SPOTS

Blind spots grow from “breaks in evidentiary links” more than from cryptography

Even visible flows cannot fully establish the facts required for taxation without knowing the taxpayer, acquisition cost, disposal proceeds, transaction purpose. The following are key bottlenecks identified in public sources.

HIGH · CRYPTOGRAPHIC

Monero

Ring signatures (ring size 16), stealth addresses, and RingCT conceal sending inputs, recipients, amounts. BTC-style deterministic flow tracing is difficult and relies on external clues such as IP, exchanges, user mistakes. No publicly verified method for complete tracing exists.

HIGH · OWNERSHIP

self-custody

Even when transfers between addresses are visible, on-chain data alone cannot readily confirm whether they are sales or transfers to self-custody, who owns the assets, or the acquisition cost, disposal proceeds. The National Tax Service (NTS) also acknowledged the difficulty of identifying all unreported transactions. View evidence

MED–HIGH · PRIVACY POOL

Zcash shielded pool

Transparent segments can be traced, but internal shielded-to-shielded paths are invisible. Time, amount boundary analysis at entry and exit is possible, but cannot confirm internal links.

MEDIUM · DATA GAP

CARF timing gap

First exchanges were reported for 2027 in Korea and some countries, 2028 in Singapore, Hong Kong, Switzerland, Canada, UAE, and 2029 in the United States. Initial gaps in overseas information exist, but do not imply long-term immunity given the assessment limitation period for offshore transactions.

MEDIUM · MODEL RISK

Heuristic misattribution

CoinJoin, service fund consolidation, and contract routing break simple rules such as “same inputs=same person.” Graph links are investigative leads and do not independently guarantee legal attribution.

MEDIUM · ENFORCEMENT

Enforceable points of contact

Exchange-custodied assets allow attachment of claims, but overseas, P2P, personal wallets may block enforcement after technical tracing without a cooperating party or private keys.

4 · Self-custody — no law regulates custody itself

Holding assets in a personal wallet does not remove tax liability on income from transfers, lending (Ministry of Finance and Economy, National Tax Service (NTS)). The prevailing interpretation treats simple transfers between one’s own wallets as non-taxable movements with no consideration paid, but no NTS notice has yet codified this.

Acquisition costs are aggregated using the total average method for each resident; the deemed acquisition cost is the higher of actual acquisition cost and market value on the day before the tax takes effect. Failure to substantiate costs may increase the tax burden, and acquisitions outside a service provider may qualify for the rule recognizing 50% of disposal proceeds as necessary expenses. Identification channels are exchange data submission, Travel Rule, CARF, foreign-exchange computer network, NTS integrated analysis system. 10 / COLLECTION

KRW 146.1 billion resulted from attachment of claims, rather than “private key hacking”

The virtual-asset-related amounts recovered through compulsory collection from 14,140 people in 2021–2024 reflect legal, operational procedures using exchanges as enforceable custodial points of contact.

Verify holdings

Identify delinquent taxpayers’ accounts, holdings from exchange-submitted data

Attach claims

Attach, freeze withdrawal claims, return claims

Recommend payment

Advise voluntary sale or separate payment

Notify of sale

Notify the delinquent taxpayer and exchange of the planned sale if they do not comply

Transfer to account

Transfer virtual assets to a tax office account

Sell at market price

Sell at that day’s market price and apply proceeds to tax arrears

Why no wallet password was needed

The attached assets were withdrawal, return claims against the exchange, rather than the on-chain private keys themselves. The exchange performs the freezing, transfer, sale.

Large-scale case in 2021

Why self-custody differs

Without exchange custody, there is no claim to attach. Address attribution, obtaining private keys, and cooperation from overseas operators become separate bottlenecks. This is an inference combining the compulsory collection framework with publicly known tracing technology.

However, the amended Civil Execution Rules (민사집행규칙), effective October 1, 2026, enabled seizure of virtual assets themselves and orders to transfer them to an enforcement officer when held directly in personal wallets. Reporting on Supreme Court announcement
